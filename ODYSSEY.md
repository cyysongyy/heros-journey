# 奧德賽 · 內在航程 — 美術清單 · Art Manifest

**模式 / Mode:** `odyssey_voyage.html` — *The Odyssey Within*

十二段冒險，各一張橫幅背景。缺圖時自動退回既有場景圖（`fb`），仍可正常遊玩。
Twelve adventures, one wide background each. Missing art auto-falls back to an existing scene image (`fb`) — the game plays regardless.

> **檔名 / Filenames** 放進 `images/`。**方向 / Orientation**：橫幅 `1536x1024`。
> **風格 / Style（每則已附）**：dark epic watercolor, warm gold + deep sea-blue palette, cinematic, mythic, painterly, no text, no lettering.

## 封面 · Cover（單獨生）→ `images/odyssey_cover.png`（正方 1024×1024 / square）

> 標題畫面的圓形徽章。單獨貼這一則，一次只生這一張。
> The circular badge on the title screen. Paste this one on its own.

```
Create a square (1024x1024) mythic watercolor illustration. Style: dark epic watercolor, warm gold and deep sea-blue palette, cinematic lighting, painterly, mythic and emotional, aged paper texture, visible brush edges. NO text, NO lettering, NO words, NO letters anywhere in the image.

IMPORTANT: Output ONE single full-bleed illustration. The painting must run all the way to all four edges — NO white paper margin, NO border, NO frame. This is NOT a storyboard, NOT a comic, NOT a contact sheet — no panels, no strips, no captions, no title.

Subject: the emblem of an inward Odyssey — a lone silhouetted figure stands in the prow of a small wooden ship at the exact center, sailing a vast circular sea ringed by a glowing golden horizon line. The left half of the sea is calm and lit with warm golden lamplight, a faint distant home-harbor glow (Ithaca, the longed-for); the right half is dark storm-tossed indigo water with faint monstrous shapes in the waves and constellations wheeling above (the unknown). A soft golden glow radiates from the figure's chest. Sacred, yearning, mythic — a ten-year voyage that is really a journey inward. Composition centered and balanced, works well cropped into a circle.
```

## 一次複製給 Gemini · One block to paste into Gemini

> 貼上後可請它「逐一生成，每張 1536×1024 橫幅」/ Paste and ask it to "generate each one, 1536×1024 landscape."

```
A cohesive set of 12 landscape (1536x1024) mythic watercolor illustrations for an inner retelling of Homer's Odyssey. Shared style for all: dark epic watercolor, warm gold and deep sea-blue palette, cinematic lighting, painterly, mythic and emotional, NO text, NO lettering, NO words. Wide aspect ratio, background art (a subject may sit off-center to leave room for overlaid text). Generate them one by one:

1. troy — The ashes of Troy at dawn: a lone Greek warrior in a dark cloak stands with his back to the smoldering, burning city, gazing out toward a distant grey sea, longing for home. Embers drifting, gold firelight against blue morning.

2. lotus — The island of the Lotus-Eaters: a hazy, honey-golden shore where dreamlike figures recline among giant glowing lotus flowers, half-dissolving into mist; one alert silhouette resists, looking back toward the ship. Seductive, narcotic, warm haze.

3. cyclops — Inside the Cyclops's cave: a huge one-eyed giant's silhouette against a cave mouth's blinding light, tiny clever men escaping beneath sheep in the shadows. Tense, dark, dramatic chiaroscuro.

4. aeolus — Aeolus's bag of winds: a small wooden ship on a moonlit sea, a leather bag bursting open releasing swirling silver storm-winds, the distant shore of home fading away. Wind, spirals, loss-in-sight.

5. circe — The sorceress Circe: an elegant enchantress in flowing robes in a torch-lit hall, swine gathered at her feet with sorrowful human eyes, magical golden vapors curling. Mystical, opulent, uneasy.

6. underworld — The Underworld: a dim ashen shore at the edge of the world, pale translucent spirits of the dead gathering around a lone living figure holding a small flame. Cold blue, ghostly gold, solemn, sacred.

7. sirens — The Sirens: a hero lashed to the mast of a ship, straining toward beautiful winged siren-women singing on jagged rocks in golden mist; crew rowing with wax-sealed ears. Yearning, dangerous, luminous.

8. scylla — Scylla and Charybdis: a small ship in a narrow strait between a towering many-headed sea-monster on one cliff and a vast churning whirlpool on the other. No safe path, dramatic, terrifying, sublime.

9. helios — The Cattle of Helios: golden sacred cattle grazing on a sunlit sacred isle under a wrathful darkening sky, a shattered ship and a lone survivor amid the storm. Divine gold turning to storm-grey.

10. calypso — Calypso's island: a lush paradise cove with a beautiful immortal goddess, and a solitary man sitting on the shore at sunset with his back to her, gazing at the sea toward distant home, tears of longing. Bittersweet, golden hour, ache of homesickness.

11. phaeacia — The court of the Phaeacians: a warm firelit banquet hall, a weathered traveler finally lowering his disguise and telling his tale to spellbound listeners; a ship waiting in the harbor beyond. Warm gold, relief, being seen.

12. ithaca — Return to Ithaca: a ragged beggar-hero drawing back a great bow that glows with golden light in his own hall, transformed, home at last, dawn breaking through the windows. Triumphant, radiant gold, homecoming, wholeness.
```
