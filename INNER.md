# 內在英雄之旅 — 美術清單 · Art Manifest

**模式 / Mode:** `inner_journey.html` — *The Inner Journey*

五站向內的旅程，各一張**直式**背景。缺圖時自動退回既有場景圖（`BGFB`），仍可正常遊玩。
Five inward stations, one **portrait** background each. Missing art auto-falls back to an existing scene image (`BGFB`) — the game plays regardless.

> **檔名 / Filenames** 放進 `images/`。**方向 / Orientation**：直式 `1024x1536`。
> **風格 / Style（每則已附）**：dark symbolic watercolor, warm gold + deep indigo-violet, dreamlike, painterly, inner/psychological, no text, no lettering.

---

## 一次複製給 Gemini · One block to paste into Gemini

> 貼上後可請它「逐一生成，每張 1024×1536 直式」/ Paste and ask it to "generate each one, 1024×1536 portrait."

```
A cohesive set of 5 vertical portrait (1024x1536) symbolic watercolor illustrations for an inner "Hero's Journey" (the journey inward). Shared style for all: dark symbolic watercolor, warm gold and deep indigo-violet palette, dreamlike, painterly, inner and psychological, cinematic soft light, NO text, NO lettering, NO words. Vertical background art (a lone figure may sit off-center to leave room for overlaid text). Generate them one by one:

1. call — A lone silhouetted figure stands in a warm, familiar lamplit room at night, turning toward a faint glowing doorway of golden light opening in the dark — a quiet, stubborn inner call. Intimate, mysterious, the first stirring of a journey.

2. depart — A solitary figure steps across a luminous threshold, leaving behind a warm golden shore of the familiar and walking into a vast deep-blue unknown; old masks and belongings dissolve into mist behind them. Letting go, crossing over, bittersweet courage.

3. wander — A small lone figure wanders a boundless, mapless dreamscape of drifting mist and floating stars, meeting a faint mirror-reflection of their own shadow-self. No path, only self facing self. Vast, uncertain, haunting yet beautiful.

4. transform — At the deepest point of the journey, a figure kneels as the old self dissolves into ash and dark water, while a new form rises glowing with golden light, like a seed cracking open or a phoenix quietly reborn. Death and rebirth, the sacred deep, profound stillness.

5. return — A transformed figure walks back into the ordinary everyday world at dawn, now carrying a soft inner golden light, becoming a gentle lamp for others; the familiar streets look the same, but the traveler is changed. Homecoming, quiet radiance, integration, hope.
```
